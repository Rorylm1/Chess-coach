CHESS COACH — IMPORTED PIECE ARTWORK

These three piece families are adapted from the artists below. Their geometry,
outline and facial/detail layers remain the artists' work. Chess Coach supplies
dynamic material colors, removes editor metadata, shadows and shared SVG IDs,
and normalizes the artwork for an inline chessboard. No endorsement is implied.
